Refund Policy

Last Updated: July 2026


1. Digital Product Policy

All products sold by PromptPro Hub are digital products delivered electronically. Once a purchase has been completed and the product has been delivered, it cannot be returned in the same way as a physical product.


2. Refund Eligibility

Refund requests may be considered if:

  • You were charged more than once for the same product.
  • You did not receive your purchased product after payment.
  • There was a verified technical issue that prevented delivery and could not be resolved.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds are generally not provided for:

  • Changing your mind after purchase.
  • Not reading the product description before buying.
  • Purchasing by mistake.
  • Expecting results that depend on your own implementation or business decisions.

4. Support Before Refund

If you experience any issue with your purchase, please contact our support team first. We will do our best to resolve the problem quickly.
